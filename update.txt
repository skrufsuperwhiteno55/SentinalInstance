# New
- Accounts page: **Show all** and **Hide all** buttons next to Play all / Close all. The Show / Hide button on each account is unchanged
- Settings > Performance > CPU limit for all accounts: set one CPU cap for every account at once (Off, 10, 25, 50 or a custom number). An account's own limit from its menu still wins
- Mini view now refreshes up to 10 times per second (Settings > Performance > Mini view > Pictures per second)

- Minimising a Roblox window while Mini view is on now keeps its picture live: the window moves off-screen (no taskbar button) and keeps being drawn. Use its Show button to bring it back. Settings > Performance > Mini view > Minimise keeps the picture live turns this off
